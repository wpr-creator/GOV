# CURRICULUM INVENTORY

## SOURCE AUTHORITY

1. California Grade Twelve Principles of American Democracy standards supplied with this project.
2. Teacher sequencing decision: complete the elections and media unit in October, before the November election.
3. The earlier pacing guide remains a planning reference, but its unit names, essential questions, and order do not control this version.

The site groups all California Government standards into eight student-facing units and keeps 38 focused topics.

| SITE UNIT | ESSENTIAL QUESTION | STANDARDS |

| Unit 0 · First Bell | Where does government show up in my life? | Course launch |

## OCTOBER ELECTION SEQUENCE

Unit 3 is marked **OCTOBER** on the student site. It includes:

- parties, primaries, caucuses, and conventions
- campaign messages, polling, advertising, and funding
- media, public opinion, and source checking
- participation beyond voting
- initiatives, referendums, and recalls
- turnout, district maps, minority voting rights, and the Electoral College

## STUDENT-LANGUAGE RULE

The site retains the California standards codes in one compact unit-level location. Lesson cards use direct actions and plain language instead of teacher-planning terms. Necessary academic vocabulary is introduced in context and supported by the glossary.
