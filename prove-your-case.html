0.6 · THE COURT IS IN SESSION

PROVE YOUR CASE

Work with a partner. Investigate a real dispute, choose a side, and defend your ruling with facts and the Constitution.

YOUR FOUR STEPS

1

CHOOSE A CASE

Work with a partner. Use one worksheet together.

2

READ THE STORY

Record who acted, what happened, and three important facts.

3

USE THE CONSTITUTION

Find the amendment. Copy its key words and explain them.

4

MAKE YOUR RULING

Choose a side. Explain how two facts and the amendment support your decision.

The Supreme Court decision stays locked until the class is ready. Your ruling may agree or disagree with the Court.
