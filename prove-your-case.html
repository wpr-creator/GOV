0.6 · THE COURT IS IN SESSION

PROVE YOUR CASE

Six real disputes. Two strong sides. Your job is to use facts and constitutional evidence to make a ruling you can defend.

HOW TO WORK A CASE

Read the story. Mark the facts that matter. Study the constitutional rule and both arguments. Then choose a ruling and record your evidence on the class worksheet.

The real Supreme Court decision stays locked until your teacher opens it. You may agree with the Court or write a reasoned dissent.
