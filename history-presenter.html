THE HISTORY LESSON

ESSENTIAL QUESTION

How did the United States replace a weak national government without creating another tyranny?

TYRANNY → WEAK GOVERNMENT → BALANCED POWER

THE HOOK

THE COMPLETE OPPOSITE

Imagine this. You date someone who decides everything—where you go, what you watch, even what pizza you order.

When it ends, you want the opposite: someone who lets you choose.

The next person never decides anything. Every answer is “Whatever you want.” Soon, you are doing all the work.

THINK · PAIR · SHAREWhy might someone choose the complete opposite after a frustrating experience?

THE BRIDGE

FROM ONE EXTREME TO ANOTHER

The complete opposite can sound perfect—until it creates a different problem.

After independence, Americans made the same kind of move. They escaped a king, created a national government that could barely act, and then searched for a better balance.

ARTICLES OF CONFEDERATIONFear of control produced a weak national government.

CONSTITUTION + RATIFICATIONAmericans asked whether stronger power could still protect liberty.
