1.03 · ARTICLE V

CHANGE THE UNCHANGEABLETRACE AN AMENDMENT FROM IDEA TO CONSTITUTION

ARTICLE V created the official steps for changing the Constitution. Change is possible, but it takes broad agreement. This stops one temporary political majority from quickly rewriting the nation’s most important rules.

THE BIG IDEA

CHANGE IS POSSIBLE.
CHANGE IS NOT EASY.

Article V separates an amendment into two jobs. First, the idea must be PROPOSED. Then the states must RATIFY, or formally approve, it.

THE KEY IDEA

ABLE TO CHANGE—BUT PROTECTED.

Article V balances two goals. The Constitution must be able to change when the country needs change. One temporary political majority should not be able to rewrite its basic rules quickly.

TWO-THIRDS PROPOSED;
